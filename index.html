What the probes measured

A single word gives its value vector. With three distinct words, the mixed output can locate their contributions inside a triangle. The seeker chooses a third word that makes the triangle easy to measure.

It collected 1,032 outputs per head across eight words. Each pair of words had a different response as the distance between them changed. One shared recurrence then forecast the seven unmeasured distances. After revealing the head, switch to “Distance only” to see how much routing the word identities explain.
